About Mavagu Tech

Practical, affordable technology solutions for small businesses and everyday users.

Mavagu Tech LLC is a technology company dedicated to delivering practical and affordable solutions for small businesses and everyday users. We specialize in software development, automation, IT services, and PC support, helping clients modernize their technology with systems that are reliable, secure, and built to scale.

Mavagu Tech began operations on January 11 and was officially formed as an LLC on January 13. On January 14, the company expanded its service offerings through the integration of ZeroLag Systems, making professional PC repairs, performance tuning, and optimization services available under the Mavagu Tech brand.

Our team combines hands-on IT experience with strong academic and professional engineering backgrounds, allowing us to bridge the gap between advanced technology and real-world usability.

Our Team

A hands-on team with software engineering, applied research, and IT support experience.

Ana Hinojosa

Founder & Managing Owner

Ana Hinojosa founded Mavagu Tech with the goal of providing accessible, high-quality technology solutions to local businesses and individuals. She brings experience as a Software Engineering Intern at Bank of America in the New York City metropolitan area and L3Harris in Colorado.

Ana graduated from Texas A&M International University in December 2024 with a degree in Computer Engineering and has an applied research background that informs the secure, scalable systems built at Mavagu Tech.

Gerardo Meza

Owner & PC Services Lead

Gerardo Meza is the owner of ZeroLag Systems, which was integrated into Mavagu Tech in January to expand PC repair, upgrade, and performance tuning services. He graduated from Texas A&M International University in May 2025 with a degree in Computer Engineering.

Gerardo has conducted academic research, including a paper titled “Deep Learning Approach for Arm Fracture Detection Based on an Improved YOLOv8 Algorithm.”

In addition to his technical work, he operates a photography side business on Instagram under @jerrymeza.cr3, blending technical skill with creative work.

Irwing De Luna

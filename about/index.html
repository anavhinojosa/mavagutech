About Mavagu Tech

Our Story

Mavagu Tech LLC is a technology company dedicated to delivering practical and affordable solutions for small businesses and everyday users. We specialize in software development, automation, IT services, and PC support, helping clients modernize their technology with systems that are reliable, secure, and built to scale.

Mavagu Tech was officially formed as an LLC on January 13, 2026. On January 14, 2026, the company expanded its service offerings through the integration of ZeroLag Systems, making professional PC repairs, performance tuning, and optimization services available under the Mavagu Tech brand.

Our team combines hands-on IT experience with strong academic and professional engineering backgrounds, allowing us to bridge the gap between advanced technology and real-world usability.

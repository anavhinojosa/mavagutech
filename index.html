About Mavagu Tech

Mavagu Tech LLC is a startup dedicated to helping small businesses modernize and grow through affordable, practical digital solutions, while also helping everyday users with PC services. Based in Laredo, Texas, our team specializesin software development, business automation, on-site IT services, and AI-powered tools designed to make everyday operations more efficient and easier to manage.

Our team brings experience as software engineers at companies such as Amazon, Bank of America, and L3Harris, along with degrees in Computer Engineering and a background in applied research. This allows us to deliver high-quality solutions to the unique needs of local businesses.
